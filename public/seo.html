Upscale

A habit, routine, and goal tracker styled after the Nintendo 3DS eShop. Build routines, group them under goals, do a daily check-in, map your progress, and unwind in a built-in arcade — as an installable PWA.

Routines and goals

Add recurring routines with a category, icon, colour, and frequency: daily, selected weekdays, or every N days. Optionally time-box a routine with an end date, link it to a long-term goal, and set a daily reminder. Goals such as “Learn piano” or “Build a website” roll up routine ratings into progress rings.

Daily check-in

Each day you rate due routines: No (priority), Not really, Kinda (counts as done), or Yes (cleared until the next scheduled date). Ratings reset each calendar day and feed streaks, heatmaps, and completion stats.

Progress mapper

Per-routine GitHub-style contribution heatmaps, current and best streaks, completion percentage, a 30-day chart, and goal-level progress rings.

Notes and reminders

Free-form notes with optional one-off reminders. Signed-in Google accounts can receive push notifications when a routine or note is due, even if the app is closed.

Arcade

Four mini-games with a seeded daily challenge and practice mode: TipTop (side-scroller), Octane (drag race), Dissiada (rhythm tiles), and Daybreak (beat-synced platformer). Personal high scores and a global daily leaderboard.

Offline-first PWA

Guest mode stores data on the device. Google sign-in adds cross-device Firestore backup. Install from the browser menu with Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app that works offline.
